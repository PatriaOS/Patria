# Patria Privacy Statement

*Early access version. Last updated October 29, 2026.*

Your family's information belongs to your family. This statement explains, in plain language, what Patria collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Patria is in early testing, so this statement will be updated as Patria grows. A full legal privacy policy will be published before Patria opens to the public.

## Our promise

- We never sell, rent or trade your information.
- We never show you advertising or use your information for advertising.
- We never use your family's information to train AI.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your name and email address. Your password is stored only in scrambled form, so Patria cannot read it.
- **Your family:** your family name, members, roles and circles.
- **What your family adds:** photos and short videos, meeting agendas, notes, tasks and replies, and Legacy Vault entries. Vault entries are locked on your phone, so Patria cannot read them.
- **Phone details needed for security:** a public key for each approved phone, and the approvals made by your family.

## What we do not collect

- **Location:** your phone removes the hidden location from photos before upload.
- **Your contacts.**
- **Your calendar:** when you add a Family Sync to your calendar, your phone's own screen adds it. Patria never reads your calendar.
- **Advertising identifiers,** and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Permissions on your phone

Patria asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it:

- **Photos:** to share the photos you choose, or to connect your library if you turn that on. Album choices stay on your phone.
- **Microphone:** only while you record a voice note.
- **Notifications:** when Patria adds notifications in a later version.

You can change these at any time in your phone's settings.

## How we use your information

- To run Patria for you and your family.
- To keep your account and your family's information secure.
- To contact you about your account and about testing.

## Who can see your information

- **Your family,** according to the circles you choose.
- **Your Vault** only by family members who hold the family key, or only by you for Only me entries.
- **Service providers** that host and run Patria, mainly Supabase (database, sign in and storage, running on Amazon Web Services in the United States). They may only use your information to provide their service to Patria.
- **The law:** we share information only if a valid legal process requires it. Vault entries remain locked, because Patria does not hold the key.

## AI

No AI reads your information today. If Patria adds optional AI features, such as the Patria Brain, each adult will choose whether to use them and how private they are. Children will be excluded, and the Vault will never be read by AI.

## Children

Children's profiles are added and managed by a parent or family admin. Patria does not knowingly collect information directly from children.

## Keeping and deleting your information

- We keep your information while your account is active.
- When someone leaves a family, their access ends at once, and their Only me Vault entries are deleted.
- You can ask us to delete your account and your information at any time by writing to us. We will confirm when it is done.

## Security

Information is encrypted while it travels and where it is stored. The Vault is locked on your phone before it leaves. Read **How Patria Protects Your Family** in Privacy and Security for the full explanation.

## Early access waitlist

If you joined the Patria waitlist, we use your email only to share progress and to invite you to testing. Reply to any update email or write to us to be removed, and we will delete your email.

## Changes and contact

We will update this statement as Patria changes and show the date at the top. Questions or requests: support.patria@gmail.com.
